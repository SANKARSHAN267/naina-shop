Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Overview

Naina's Shop is a lightweight browser extension that displays a product popup and static contact information. This privacy policy explains what data (if any) the extension collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

The extension does not collect personal data or send data to remote servers. It uses the Chrome storage API only to save optional developer-configured settings locally on the user's device (for example: store name, notification preferences). That data remains on the user's device.

Contact Information

The email and Discord details shown in the extension are static content provided by the developer. They are not collected from users.

Third-Party Services

The extension does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes

The developer may update this privacy policy. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date will be revised.
